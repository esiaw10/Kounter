KOUNTER USER GUIDE

How to use Kounter

Follow these steps to record sales, manage stock and review your shop's performance. Some tools are available only to store managers and store admins.

POS Stock management Sales reports Profit analysis Attendants Subscriptions Account help

1. Make a sale with POS

Managers, store admins and attendants
  1. Open POS from the menu or dashboard.
  2. Find the product using the search box or category filter. Use its barcode where the POS supports barcode entry.
  3. Add the product to the cart and set the quantity the customer is buying. Repeat for other items.
  4. Check the products, quantities and total, then review the sale.
  5. Select Complete Sale and wait for the success confirmation.

A completed sale reduces stock and records who made the sale. If an error appears, read it before trying again. Do not submit the same sale twice.

2. Manage products and stock

Product changes and restocking: managers and store admins
  1. Choose Add Product. Enter the product name, category, cost price, selling price and opening stock.
  2. Set the low-stock threshold and enter a barcode if you use one. Save the product.
  3. Open Active Products to check available products and quantities.
  4. Use the product's edit option to update its details or prices.
  5. When supplies arrive, open Restock Stock, select the product, enter the quantity received and submit.

Cost price is what the shop pays per item. Selling price is what the customer pays. Review low-stock alerts before an item runs out. Deactivating a product removes it from active selling; it does not mean it has been sold.

3. View and print sales

  1. Open Sales History / Daily Sales and choose a date to review that day's sales.
  2. Open All Sales, or click Total Sales on the dashboard, to view the shop's sales history.
  3. Check the date and time, product, quantity, price, total and Sold By for each sale.
  4. Use the Print button on All Sales to print the report or save it as a PDF through your browser's print window.

Managers can reset sales using the password-confirmation tool. Reset permanently removes sales history and changes report totals. Save any reports you need before resetting.

4. Understand your profit

Managers and store admins

Open All Sales and find Profit Analysis.

  • Revenue: the total value of recorded sales.
  • Cost of goods sold: the cost of the items sold, multiplied by their quantities.
  • Gross profit: revenue minus cost of goods sold.
  • Profit margin: gross profit divided by revenue, multiplied by 100.

For example, revenue of GH₵100 and a cost of GH₵70 give GH₵30 gross profit and a 30% margin. Expand View profit for each sale for the breakdown.

Older sales without a saved cost are marked as estimates using current product costs. New sales save their original cost. Gross profit excludes rent, wages, taxes and other expenses.

5. Create and manage attendants

Managers and store admins
  1. On the dashboard, select Manage Attendants to open the panel.
  2. Expand Create attendant account. Enter a unique username, optional name and email, and matching passwords.
  3. Create the account. Give the attendant their login details privately; they sign in on the same login page.
  4. Use Edit details or Reset password beside an account when needed.
  5. Deactivate blocks login. Activate restores access. Delete permanently removes the account after confirmation.

Attendants belong to your store. Deleting an account retains recorded sales and the saved Sold By name. The creation form closes after a successful submission.

6. Check your subscription

Managers and store admins
  1. Open Subscription from the navigation's subscription indicator.
  2. Check whether the store is on its free trial, has an active subscription, or needs renewal.
  3. Follow the payment instructions on the subscription page when renewal is needed.
  4. Return to Kounter and confirm that the subscription is active after payment.

New stores receive a 30-day trial. Product management is restricted once both the trial and paid subscription have expired. If payment is not reflected, check the payment result before paying again.

7. Login and account help

  • Use your own account so sales are credited to the correct person. Log out when you finish using a shared device.
  • If login fails, check your username and password. A deactivated attendant with correct credentials sees a message explaining that their manager deactivated the account.
  • Ask your store manager to reactivate your account or reset your password. Use the login page's password-reset option if your account has a working email address.
  • Open Profile to manage your personal account details and password.
  • If a sale, stock update or payment fails, keep the error message and ask your manager for help before repeating the action.
