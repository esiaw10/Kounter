Frequently asked questions

A few things to know.

Do I need to install anything?

No installation is needed. Open Kounter in your web browser on your phone, tablet or computer.

How does the free trial work?

Your shop receives a 30-day trial. After it expires, a paid subscription is required to continue product management. Available plans are shown in your account.

Can my attendants use it?

Yes. Shop managers can create attendant accounts and manage their access.

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes. An internet connection is required to access your shop and save updates.

Can I add many products at once?

Yes. Download the product spreadsheet template, fill in your products and use the bulk upload feature.
